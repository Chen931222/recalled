    What the answers mean

    Likely recalled
    A model number or UPC you gave is listed in a recall notice, or the notice covers all models of the product line you named. Stop using it and follow the notice.
    Possible match
    Same brand and kind of product, but nothing you gave pins it to the recalled units. Compare the models, date codes or description in the notice.
    No match found
    None of the notices searched list it. That is not the same as safe: this covers only U.S. and Canadian notices for baby and kids gear.
